No signal in the basement. You send ₹500. Your phone encrypts it, hands it to whoever's nearby, and it hops device to device until one phone walks outside and quietly uploads it. This console drives that whole path by hand — one hop at a time — against a live backend.

01 Compose a payment

Simulates the sender's own phone: builds the instruction, encrypts it with the server's public key, hands it to phone-alice.

02 Gossip & bridge

Each round, every device broadcasts its packets to everyone in range. TTL drops per hop. Only phone-bridge has signal.
